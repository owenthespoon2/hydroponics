# 🖨️ Hardware & 3D-Printed Parts

This directory is reserved for mechanical CAD files (`.stl`, `.step`, `.f3d`) and enclosure designs as the project scales.

## Planned Designs
* **Jar Lid Net-Pot Adapters:** Custom 3D-printed ring inserts for 1.3L and 2.5L jars to hold 30 mm / 40 mm sponges cleanly without cutting lids.
* **DS18B20 Probe Clip:** Snap-on rim clip to suspend the waterproof stainless temperature probe mid-depth in the 35×25×16 cm water bath without touching the heater or tub floor.
* **Pi Camera & Relay Enclosure:** Splash-resistant desk mount holding the Raspberry Pi, 5V relay module, and USB Type-A breakout board neatly beside the bath.
