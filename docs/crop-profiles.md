# Crop profiles and growing parameters

Working recipes per crop, using **Hyponica A and B**. These are starting points. Update a profile when a run teaches me something.

**Nutrient strength:** the earlier plan used **1:500** (2 ml A + 2 ml B per 1,000 ml water) for all crops. Check this against the bottle label before relying on it, and record what I actually use in each post.

---

## 1. Tomato: Premium Ruby (プレミアムルビー) — Run 1
- **What it is:** F1 cherry tomato (ミニトマト) from Kaneko Seeds. Fruit about 15–20 g. Sets trusses, with compound trusses from the middle of the plant upward. The seller says it suits unheated and rain-shelter greenhouse growing.
- **Plant habit:** the seller's page does not give plant height or habit. Plan on a tall vining plant until the run shows otherwise.
- **Nutrition note from the seller:** avoid excess nitrogen; too much vigour or extreme heat causes misshapen fruit.
- **Container:** started in 1.3 L. Expect to need a much larger reservoir (5 L or more) if it thrives.
- **Lighting:** 14–16 hours a day, strong direct coverage.
- **Root temperature:** about 20–24 °C.
- **Pollination:** indoors there is no wind or bees. Tap or vibrate the flower clusters every few days once flowers open.
- **Source:** [Kaneko Seeds Premium Ruby listing](https://www.tanehyo.jp/view/item/000000006401)

## 2. Micro-dwarf tomato (Regina / Micro Tom) — candidate
- Stays 15–30 cm, suited to small containers.
- 2–3 L light-blocked container. Same light and temperature as above.

## 3. Sweet basil (スイートバジル) — waiting on fresh seed
- Container: 1.3 L light-blocked jar.
- Sowing: shallow 5–8 mm pocket in the sponge, 2–3 seeds, bottom 5–10 mm of the sponge submerged. Thin to the strongest seedling at the first true leaves.
- Lighting: 12–14 hours a day, 15–25 cm above the canopy.
- Root temperature: 20–25 °C.
- Harvest: pinch above a leaf node once there are 3–4 pairs of true leaves. Take at most a third of the leaves at once.

## 4. Bell pepper (パプリカ・ピーマン) — future
- 5–10 L reservoir. 90–120+ days from seed to ripe fruit. Grows into a 60–90 cm woody bush.

## 5. Rosemary (ローズマリー) — future
- 2–3 L. Seeds germinate poorly and slowly; cuttings in plain water first work better. Slightly lighter feeding than tomatoes.

## 6. Green beans (インゲン) — future
- 5 L or more. Choose a bush variety and give it a small support frame.
